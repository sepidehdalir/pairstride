BUILT WITH CARE

A small tool.
A deliberate pipeline.

Pairstride uses SwiftUI, AVFoundation, Core Image, Core Text, PhotosUI and StoreKit 2. One composition and renderer drive preview and export. Automated checks independently decode paired event frames, measure selected-source audio, exercise cancellation, verify source hashes and reopen owned project copies. Native and device QA completed before submission. Pairstride 1.0 (1) and Lifetime Pro were submitted together on October 20, 2026 and are Waiting for Review.

Input: H.264 or HEVC SDR in MP4/MOV, up to 120 seconds, 250 MB, 4096 pixels per side and 120 fps per clip. Output is re-encoded 30 fps SDR. Manual alignment is a viewing aid; it does not measure technique, diagnose movement or automatically match frames.
