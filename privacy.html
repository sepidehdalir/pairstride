PRIVACY / OCTOBER 19, 2026

Your clips.
Your device.

Pairstride processes selected videos on your iPhone or iPad. The developer does not collect your videos, comparison projects, identifiers, usage analytics or tracking data through the app. There is no developer account, processing server, advertising SDK or external AI service.

Selected videos and local files

The system Photos picker or Files picker lets you choose clips. Pairstride makes local working copies and creates a separate comparison movie; it does not overwrite your source clips. Working sessions and exported movies are temporary and are cleared on a later launch. Save a finished movie to Files or Photos before closing the app.

Optional Pro projects keep owned source copies, alignment markers and output settings in the app’s local library. Projects can be moved to recoverable Trash, restored or permanently removed. Saved and trashed media share a 2 GB limit. Presets store your labels and output settings locally. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Apple device backup may include the saved library depending on your device settings; Pairstride does not provide its own cloud sync.

Permissions and sharing

Pairstride requests add-only Photos access only when you choose to add a new exported movie to Photos. You can decline and use Files instead. The app does not request camera, microphone, location or full Photos-library read access. Sharing a movie or saving to a cloud-backed Files location uses the destination you choose and its own privacy practices.

Purchases

Apple manages payments and purchase history. Pairstride uses StoreKit to check your verified Pro entitlement and restore purchases. The developer does not receive your payment card details.

Support and this website

If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to send, including your email address. Avoid sending private videos unless you intend to share them. This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process ordinary website request information under its own privacy policy. The site contains no developer analytics or advertising scripts.
